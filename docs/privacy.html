Lift privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Lift is a workout log for iPhone. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What Lift stores

Your routines, exercises, notes and logged sets are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is on for your device, Lift also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud database so you can restore it. We can't see or access that data.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Lift saves each finished workout to Apple Health as Traditional Strength Training. Lift does not read other data from Health. Health data is never used for advertising and never leaves your devices except through Apple's own Health and iCloud services.

Notifications

Rest-timer notifications are scheduled on your iPhone. No notification content is sent to us.

Sharing

Lift sends no data to the developer or to any third party. CSV export only happens when you choose it, and you decide where the file goes.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove its data from your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Lift.
